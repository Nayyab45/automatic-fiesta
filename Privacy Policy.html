Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2023

Please read this privacy policy carefully before using the What Should We Eat? application. Your privacy and trust are paramount to our curated dining experience.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a tailored culinary concierge service, we collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, update your profile, or interact with our concierge features.

  • Profile Data: Name, email address, phone number, and dining preferences (e.g., dietary restrictions, favorite cuisines).
  • Interaction Data: Reviews, ratings, and reservations made through the platform.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is utilized exclusively to enhance your dining experience and refine our recommendations. We do not sell your personal data to third-party marketers.

  • To personalize your feed with relevant restaurant suggestions.
  • To facilitate reservations and communicate with our partner venues.
  • To send essential service updates and curated invitations (with your consent).

3. Location Services

What Should We Eat? relies on location data to suggest nearby dining experiences.

We only access your location while the app is in use or running in the background, provided you have granted explicit permission through your device settings. You can revoke this access at any time, though it may limit the app's functionality.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Our servers use encryption protocols to safeguard data during transmission and storage.

However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security.
