1. Profile Standards

To foster a genuine and trustworthy community, all members are expected to maintain accurate and appropriate profiles.

  1. Authenticity: You must use your real name or a recognizable alias. Profile photos must clearly represent you or reflect your genuine culinary interests without violating any local modesty standards.
  2. Appropriate Content: Bios and photos must be free from offensive, explicit, or politically sensitive material. This platform is dedicated to the celebration of food and culture.
  3. No Impersonation: Creating accounts intended to mimic other members, restaurants, or public figures is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate suspension.

2. Conduct at Meetups

Our meetups are designed to be inclusive, respectful, and focused on the shared joy of dining.

  1. Punctuality and Attendance: Please respect the time of your hosts and fellow diners. If you RSVP, you are expected to attend. Repeated no-shows may limit your ability to join future events.
  2. Respectful Interaction: Engage in courteous conversation. Harassment, discrimination, or aggressive behavior toward fellow members or restaurant staff will not be tolerated.
  3. Financial Responsibility: Be prepared to cover your portion of the bill as agreed upon by the group prior to the meal. Disputes over minor amounts should be handled gracefully.

3. Safety Requirements

The physical and digital safety of our members is our highest priority.

  1. Meeting in Public: We strongly advise that all community meetups occur in public restaurants or established dining venues.
  2. Privacy Protection: Do not share personal contact information, home addresses, or financial details in public forums or group chats.
  3. Allergy Awareness: While organizing, communicate clearly about severe food allergies. However, individuals are ultimately responsible for verifying ingredients with the venue.

4. Prohibited Behavior

The following actions violate the core spirit of the Dastarkhān community.

  1. Spam and Solicitation: Using the platform to sell products, promote unrelated services, or spam members is forbidden.
  2. Hate Speech and Harassment: Any form of hate speech, bullying, or targeted harassment based on race, religion, gender, or background.
  3. Platform Manipulation: Creating fake reviews, artificially boosting ratings, or exploiting the app's features for personal gain.
